Your question
Why doesn’t a larger learning rate always help gradient descent converge faster?
✦ EduTutor AI3 sources used

A larger learning rate makes each update bigger, not necessarily better. If the step is too large, it can jump past the minimum, causing oscillation or even divergence. [1]

θt+1 = θt − α ∇J(θt)α = learning rate [2]
Small enough: moves toward the minimum
Too large: overshoots the minimum
